# FundMeOut — Brand Strategy & Positioning

## Essence
**Tagline:** "Giving with a joyful smile."  
**Manifesto (Slide 01):** "People. Causes. A Brighter Tomorrow."  
**Closing sign-off (Slide 16):** "Real People. Real Impact."

FundMeOut lifts someone *out* of hardship — the name is the promise. The smile does the work: gathering community around the "O" and flicking up past "ut".

## Audience
- **Primary: Donors 25-45** — mobile-first, cause-driven, skepticism about transparency. Need 1-tap donation, live impact tracking, social proof in <15s.
- **Secondary: Creators/campaign owners** — need trust, tools (post-update, transfers, trust-shield), and amplification (share, causes).

## Category & Competitors
GoFundMe (default), Kickstarter (creation), Patreon (membership), Givebutter (modern). FundMeOut wins on **joyful precision + hardware tactility** — not another blue fintech, but a collectible companion.

## Voice
Playful yet sophisticated. Bold, not bubbly. Swiss short sentences, generous space. Never jargon. Every claim shows a number or a face.

## Positioning Statement
For people who want their giving to feel *good* and *proven*, FundMeOut is the creative tech platform that pairs transparent crowdfunding with a tactile, smiling companion — so small actions become big dreams.

## Pillars → Expression
1. **Playful Yet Sophisticated** → Swiss grid + electric cobalt.
2. **Smile Gesture** → Wordmark "O" smile, u-swoop, Pebble halo, loaders.
3. **Tactile Obsession** → Matte grain, cyan rim, braided cable, die-cut lime sleeve.
4. **Surreal Optimism** → Iridescent spheres, holographic companion, optimistic editorial.
