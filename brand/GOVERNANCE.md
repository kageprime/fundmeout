# FundMeOut — Brand Governance & Usage Guide

## Who Can Use What
- **Internal:** Full system — `brand/tokens.json`, `logos/`, `icons/`.
- **Partners:** White and monochrome only, with clear space. No custom colorways.
- **Community/press:** App icon + wordmark only, no FMO mark solo.

## Misuse — Never
- Alter smile curvature or detach from O/u.
- Place primary `#0052FF` wordmark on lime or cyan.
- Use lime `#06FF2E` with white text (1.37:1 fail) — always charcoal `#06090E` text.
- Stretch, skew, add drop shadow beyond `--shadow-glass`.
- Invent new radii or #hex outside `brand/tokens.json`.

## Approval Workflow
1. Draft in `brand/DESIGN.md` tokens.
2. Review via `reviewing-interface-quality` audit (desktop+mobile screenshot + distinct-value counts).
3. A11y gate: `building-accessible-interfaces` contrast + keyboard walk.
4. Merge only after `brand/MANIFEST.md` checklist green.
