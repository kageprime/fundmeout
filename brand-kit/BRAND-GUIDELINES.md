# FundMeOut — Brand Identity & Design System Kit

> **"Small device. Big impact."**  
> *A creative tech platform for real people making a real impact.*

---

## 1. Brand Essence & Visual Language
FundMeOut merges the tactile, playful joy of high-concept toy industrial design (inspired by Teenage Engineering, Nothing, and contemporary Japanese product design) with the refined sophistication of an art-directed creative studio.

### Key Tenets:
1. **Playful Yet Sophisticated**: Bold energetic color blocking balanced by disciplined Swiss typography.
2. **The Smile Gesture**: The smiling arc acts as a universal motif across hardware LEDs, packaging die-cuts, kinetic UI animations, and typography.
3. **Tactile Product Obsession**: Matte soft-touch finishes, glowing circular LED feedback, braided cables, and collector-grade unboxing.
4. **Surreal Optimism**: Editorial photography with vibrant iridescent spheres, cinematic light, and holographic feedback.

## 2. Brand Architecture & 16-Slide System

### Slide 01 / 16: Brand Cover & Manifesto
- **Headline**: "People. Causes. A Brighter Tomorrow."
- **Visuals**: Vibrant electric blue background with crisp white typography and the smile curve emblem.

### Slide 02 / 16: Logo System — 4 Options (no hierarchy)
- **Option 01 — Brand Guidelines**: `FundMe☺ut` with centered smile in "O" + swooping "u" — `logos/official-brand-primary.svg` (+ white/mono/FMO/loader).
- **Option 02 — Uplift Bridge**: Smile sweeps under "O" into "ut" with lift flick — `logos/concept-2-smile-bridge.svg`.
- **Option 03 — Joyful 'O'**: Arch eyes + smile inside "O" — `logos/concept-1-smiley-o.svg`.
- **Option 04 — Halo Badge**: Standalone tactile emblem + wordmark — `logos/concept-3-fund-badge.svg`.
- **Note:** All 4 are equal options in `logos.html` — no selected canonical.

### Slide 03 / 16: Brand Narrative & Editorial
- **Headline**: "Small actions. Big dreams."
- **Story**: Where grassroots community funding meets futuristic creative technology.

### Slide 04 / 16: Color & Typography — Locked Tokens (single source of truth)
- **Primary Electric Blue**: `#0052FF` (Primary brand color, digital hero — canonical; `#0047FF` deprecated)
- **Vivid Cyan**: `#00F0FF` (Secondary accent, LED halo, gradients)
- **Electric Lime**: `#06FF2E` (Action accent, high-energy highlights — `#D6FF47` retired as alternate lime)
- **Sunset Pink**: `#FF408A` (Community warmth, emotional resonance)
- **Deep Purple**: `#7B30FF` (Editorial contrast, evening events)
- **Soft White**: `#F7F9FF` (Clean paper background, card surfaces)
- **Charcoal Void**: `#08080F` (Dark mode foundation, hardware body)
- **Typography**: **Satoshi** (Bold, Medium, Regular) primary display + **Plus Jakarta Sans** (loaded via Google Fonts) for UI headers + **JetBrains Mono** for mono tags. SVGs with `@import` must be outlined for print/offline.

### Slide 05 / 16: Stylized Oval Iconography
- **Grid**: 24×24px, 2px stroke, rounded caps and joins.
- **Categories**: Navigation (User, Profile, Browse), Causes (Animals, Education, Medical, Community), and Finance (Donations, Transfers, Trust Shield, Post Update).

### Slide 06 / 16: Hardware Product Obsession
- **The FundMeOut Pebble**: A circular tactile companion puck with a continuous 360° glowing cyan smile LED rim, capacitive touch face, and USB-C port.

### Slide 07 / 16: Lifestyle & Photography
- Art-directed editorial portraits showcasing diverse changemakers and dreamers holding the glowing device.

### Slide 08 / 16: CMF (Color, Material, Finish)
- **Midnight Black**: Textured micro-grain matte finish with cyan smile light.
- **Cloud White**: Ceramic-inspired matte soft white with cobalt smile indicator.
- **Electric Sky Blue**: Limited-edition high-gloss anodized rim with neon green accent.

### Slide 09 / 16: Tactile Packaging & Unboxing
- Two-piece rigid box in electric blue, neon lime die-cut sleeve, custom molded inner tray, braided USB-C cable, quick-start cards, and sticker sheets.

### Slide 10 / 16: Brand Magazine & Editorial Spread
- High-fashion editorial layouts ("Ideas People Change") with graphic curves and typographic rhythm.

### Slide 11 / 16: Digital App Experience
- iOS & Android native mobile design: Splash screen smile loading sequence, intuitive category browsing, and 1-tap transparent donation checkout.

### Slide 12 / 16: Immersive Retail & Spatial Pop-Up
- Flagship spatial design featuring a suspended circular smile ceiling halo, rounded interaction plinths, and neon impact walls.

### Slide 13 / 16: Motion Design & Kinetic Smile
- Dot → Crescent → Full Joyful Arc motion choreography responding dynamically to donations and goal milestones.

### Slide 14 / 16: Modular Social Campaign
- High-contrast 1:1 and 4:5 social assets pairing vibrant lime cards with community portraits.

### Slide 15 / 16: Out-of-Home Urban Billboards
- Large-scale architectural LED billboard installations in creative tech capitals.

### Slide 16 / 16: Closing Sign-Off
- **"Real People. Real Impact."** — Floating liquid chrome spheres and signature smile mark.
